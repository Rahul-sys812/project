    Installation & Maintenance Tips

    Installation Best Practices:

    1. Clean the installation surface thoroughly before placement
    2. Follow manufacturer guidelines for spacing and positioning
    3. Use appropriate fasteners for permanent installations
    4. Ensure proper alignment for modular products
    5. Mark installation areas clearly for safety

    Maintenance Guidelines:

    1. Regular visual inspections for wear and damage
    2. Clean products periodically to maintain visibility and grip
    3. Replace damaged units immediately to maintain safety
    4. Check fasteners and connections regularly
    5. Store spare units in dry, cool conditions
